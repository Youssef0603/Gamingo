Support

How can we help?

Laglingo helps players learn, translate, listen to, and practice useful gaming phrases across multiple languages.

Speech practice

Open a phrase and tap Practice. Allow microphone and speech-recognition access when prompted, then say the displayed phrase.

Speech recognition availability and accuracy can vary by device, language, network connection, and operating-system speech service.

Languages and phrases

Use the Native and Learning selectors on the Practice screen to change languages. Tap Add to create a custom translated word.

Favourites

Tap the heart button on a phrase to save it. Saved phrases appear in the Favourites tab and are stored locally on your device.

Ads

Laglingo uses non-personalized banner and interstitial ads to support the app. Ads do not appear during the initial first-use grace period.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when available.
